# NOEXCUSE 💪

A simple, beginner-friendly home fitness and nutrition companion. No gym, no sign-up, no server. It runs entirely in your browser and works offline.

## Features
- **Home workouts:** about 30-minute, equipment-free sessions with step-by-step instructions, sets, reps, rest timers and YouTube technique links. They get harder as you progress.
- **Smart scheduler:** tell it how your day went and how much energy you have. It picks a gentle, light or full workout. Missed sessions can be rescheduled without guilt.
- **Calorie and protein tracker:** personalised targets, meal logging, remaining allowance and a 7-day chart.
- **Craving assistant:** healthier versions of pizza, burgers, sweets, fried snacks, biryani and more.
- **Cooking assistant:** make Maggi, paneer sabzi, dal, poha and others healthier, and see calories and protein before and after.
- **Progress tracking:** weekly consistency, weight trend and encouraging, realistic feedback.

## Use it
Open the live site in your phone browser. In Chrome choose **⋮ → Add to Home screen** to install it like an app.

## Privacy
All data is stored only in your browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent anywhere. Use **Settings → Download backup** to save a copy.

## Run locally
Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. There is nothing to build or install.

## Disclaimer
General wellness guidance only, not medical advice. Calorie and nutrition values are approximate. If you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant, check with your doctor before starting.
